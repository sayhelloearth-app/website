Privacy

Effective November 25, 2026

Hello Earth is a calm nature puzzle app for children ages 2 to 6, coming soon. This page covers this website and its email list. The app will have its own privacy policy when it launches.

What we collect

Only the email address a grown-up types into the signup. We don’t ask for a name, a child’s name or anything about a child. This site uses no cookies, ads or analytics.

How we use it

To tell you when Hello Earth launches, and to send an occasional hello when new puzzles arrive. We never sell or rent your email address, and we don’t share it with advertisers.

Where it’s kept

Signups are stored with Google, in our own Google account. We keep your address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security, under GitHub’s own privacy statement.

Children

The signup is for parents, grandparents and other grown-ups. We don’t knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child signed up, write to us and we’ll delete it.

Your choices

Every email we send includes a way to unsubscribe. To see, change or delete the address we hold, write to hello@sayhelloearth.com.

Do Not Track

This site doesn’t track you across other sites, so it works the same whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

Changes

If this page changes, we’ll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your email address, we’ll tell you by email first.
